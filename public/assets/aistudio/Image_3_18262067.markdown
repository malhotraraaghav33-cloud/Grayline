## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a high-reliability, low-distraction tactical computing environment engineered for emergency triage teams, combat medics, and aeromedical evacuation coordinators operating under harsh physical constraints and severe sensory stress. 

The aesthetic is derived from MIL-STD rugged field terminals and tactical battle management displays: utilitarian, data-dense, structured, and impervious to ambient visual noise. Visual hierarchy must prioritize rapid glanceability and rapid status classification. Decorative flourishes, excessive radii, dramatic drop shadows, and non-essential animations are strictly eliminated. 

Interface elements reflect the mechanical precision of military instrumentation: crisp 1px structural delineations, mono-spaced positional telemetry, clear compartmentalized data bays, and a muted olive-drab and dark charcoal surface system accented by purposeful triage signifiers.

## Colors
The color architecture relies strictly on an analog optical discipline: night-adapted, glare-resistant surfaces anchored by earth-toned chromas. 

Under no circumstances may digital primary blues, cyans, magentas, or neon purple chromatic ramps be introduced. Every pixel must serve situational clarity.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#171A16`):** The absolute foundational background. Absorbs peripheral light and grounds the screen.
- **Main Terminal Background (`#20241F`):** Standard page surface and structural viewport backing.
- **Panel Surface (`#252A23`):** Primary container for modules, flight lists, triage queues, and telemetry streams.
- **Elevated / Active Surface (`#2D332A`):** Raised states, floating modals, contextual sub-panels, and active hover tiers.
- **Divider & Framing Stroke (`#41473A`):** Structural 1px border that physically delineates operational sectors.

### Chromatic Accents & Navigation
- **Primary Action / Active Nav (`#596447` - Tactical Olive):** High-priority state indicators, selected tabs, confirm buttons.
- **Sub-Primary / Inactive Active (`#343D2C` - Deep Olive):** Toggle backs, recessed status tracks, nested containers.
- **Field Highlight (`#788365` - Field Green):** Subtle iconography, secondary interactive highlights, focus rings.

### Typography & Readability
- **Primary Data / Readings (`#E8E5D9` - Warm Off-White):** Maximum readable text for vitals, patient identifiers, and critical directives.
- **Secondary Data / Units (`#A9AE9F` - Muted Secondary Text):** Metadata labels, units of measure (mmHg, bpm), grid column headers.
- **Accent Callouts (`#B9AD8D` - Tactical Khaki) & (`#D6C9A8` - Field Sand):** Sector indicators, aircraft callsigns, evacuation priority codes.

### Triage & Telemetry Status Signifiers
Status signals override default palettes and must be treated as absolute state warnings:
- **Emergency / Priority 1 / Immediate (`#C6534D`):** Arterial bleeding, airway failure, severe trauma. Used on tags, edge badges, and critical warning alerts.
- **Delayed / Priority 2 / Urgency (`#D4A44D`):** Moderate shock, fractured limbs, systemic monitoring required.
- **Minimal / Priority 3 / Stable (`#83A17A`):** Ambulatory, controlled vitals, routine transport queue.

## Typography
Typographic treatment follows strict split-discipline execution:

1. **Analytical / Structural (IBM Plex Sans):** Applied across global platform navigation, patient demographic dossiers, narrative trauma notes, and standard command buttons. It provides high-contrast legibility even on low-nit vehicle consoles.
2. **Telemetry / Quantitative (Space Mono):** Mandatory for all spatial coordinates (MGRS/GPS), timestamps (ZULU/LOCAL), physiological vital sign monitors (HR, SpO2, NIBP), transport unit callsigns, and patient triage barcodes. Mono-spaced metrics eliminate layout jitter caused by real-time streaming numerical changes.

### Typographic Rules
- All categorical labels, metadata keys, and triage priority tags must render in uppercase with positive letter spacing (`0.05em` to `0.06em`).
- Numerical telemetry readouts must maintain rigid vertical grid alignment. Never use proportional figures for dynamic vital streams.
- Mobile display downscales `headline-xl` to 24px and `headline-lg` to 20px to prevent viewport truncation inside cramped field vehicle tablets.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout reflects a multi-pane command and control environment designed for split-second parsing and high data density. Space is treated as a finite tactical asset; gratuitous margins and airy white space are actively suppressed in favor of compact, deliberate structural grouping.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column variable grid on desktop operations centers; 4-column rigid layout on rugged mobile field tablets.
- **Docked Operational Bins:** The screen is divided into fixed architectural zones: Mission Navigation Bar (top/left), Active Sector/Flight Tracker (center or side panel), Incident Queue (main workspace), and Live Vitals/Telemetry Deck (right/docked rail).
- **Density Profile:** Compact. Inter-card spacing strictly utilizes `0.5rem` (`space-sm`) to `0.75rem` (`space-md`). Padding inside telemetry cells rarely exceeds `0.5rem` to keep multiple casualty trajectories simultaneously visible without vertical scrolling.
